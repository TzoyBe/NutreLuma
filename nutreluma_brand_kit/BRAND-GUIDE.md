# NutreLuma Brand Guide

## Brand idea
NutreLuma combines nutrition, clarity and progress. The symbol resembles four leaves/petals around a central point, suggesting balance, food, health, focus and intelligent analysis.

## Primary tagline
**See your food differently.**

## Alternative taglines
- Snap. Understand. Progress.
- Smarter nutrition, one meal at a time.
- From plate to insight.
- Your meals, made clearer.

## Primary colors

- Deep Teal 700 — `#0B5B57`

## Typography
Recommended:
- **Headings:** Inter / Manrope, 600–700
- **Body:** Inter / Manrope, 400–500
- **Numbers and nutrition metrics:** Inter, 600–700 with tabular numerals

## Logo usage
- Use the gradient version on white, mint or dark navy backgrounds.
- Use the white monochrome version on photography or very dark backgrounds.
- Keep clear space equal to the diameter of the center dot around the logo.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual petals or add shadows to the symbol.
- For favicon and app icons, use the standalone symbol.
- For website headers, use the horizontal wordmark.

## UI recommendations
- Primary button: gradient or Forest 600 background with white text.
- Secondary button: Mint 50 background, Forest 600 text.
- Cards: white or Slate 950 depending on theme, 20px radius.
- Progress indicators: Emerald 400 → Forest 600 gradient.
- Health warnings: use amber; avoid using red for ordinary calorie overages.

## Brand tone
Clear, supportive, modern and non-judgmental. Avoid language that labels food as “bad”, “cheat” or “failure”.
