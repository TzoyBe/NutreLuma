Nutreluma Brand Guidelines — v2

**Parent brand:** Joybee Digital

**Product brand:** Nutreluma

**Tagline:** **Nutrition that learns you.**

1. Brand idea

Nutreluma is an AI nutrition product that learns from the user's own meal history, corrections, goals and progress. The visual identity should feel intelligent, premium, calm and personal — not clinical and not like a generic diet app.

2. Parent-brand relationship

Nutreluma inherits Joybee Digital's core visual DNA:

  • Navy `#0B1020`
  • Blue `#2563EB`
  • Purple `#7C3AED`
  • Gold `#FFB703`
  • Teal `#10B981`
  • Sora for headings
  • Inter for UI/body

    • Use **“A Joybee Digital product”** only in About, press, footer, legal and corporate contexts. It should not compete with the Nutreluma name in primary consumer-facing UI.

    3. Signature visual

    The Nutreluma logo uses the gold → blue → violet spectrum as its distinctive product signature. This gradient should be reserved for:

  • logo/ring accents
  • premium/high-value moments
  • selected hero graphics
  • achievement or intelligence states

    • Do not make every UI control a multicolor gradient.

    4. Core colors

  • Navy: `#0B1020` — primary background
  • Blue: `#2563EB` — main action / product intelligence
  • Purple: `#7C3AED` — personalization / premium accent
  • Gold: `#FFB703` — milestones / highlights
  • Teal: `#10B981` — positive health/status signal
  • Light: `#F3F4F6` — light surfaces
  • White: `#FFFFFF` — text on dark surfaces

    • 5. Nutrition data colors

    Maintain consistent data semantics:

  • Calories: `#3B6FF5`
  • Protein: `#38BDF8`
  • Carbohydrates: `#FFB703`
  • Fat: `#A855F7`
  • Fibre: `#10B981`

    • Do not change these per screen.

    6. Typography

    Recommended:

  • Headings / brand: **Sora 600–700**
  • Body / UI: **Inter 400–600**
  • Metrics: **Inter 600–700**, use tabular numerals where supported

    • Font files are intentionally not bundled. Obtain Sora and Inter from their official distributors.

    7. Logo usage

  • Use the standalone mark for favicon, app icon, avatar and small UI.
  • Use the horizontal wordmark in website headers and marketing material.
  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 10% of its width.
  • Do not rotate the logo.
  • Do not recolor individual blades/petals.
  • Do not add heavy drop shadows.
  • Do not place the full-color logo on visually noisy photography without a dark/neutral container.

    • 8. UI direction

  • Dark-first product experience
  • Glass/frosted cards are allowed but should remain readable
  • 24px primary card radius
  • 16px control radius
  • Blue is the primary CTA color
  • Gold is an accent, not a general CTA color
  • Purple indicates personalization, premium intelligence or selected states
  • Teal is reserved for successful/healthy status where useful

    • 9. Tone of voice

    Nutreluma should sound:

  • calm
  • intelligent
  • supportive
  • concise
  • non-judgmental

    • Prefer:

  • “Estimated”
  • “Based on your tracked data”
  • “Your recent pattern”
  • “See how this fits your day”

    • Avoid:

  • “Bad food”
  • “Cheat meal”
  • “Failure”
  • “Exact calories”
  • “Guaranteed weight loss”

    • 10. Core product lines

    Primary:

    **Nutrition that learns you.**

    Secondary:

  • **See your food differently.**
  • **From plate to insight.**
  • **Track smarter. Learn your patterns.**
  • **AI nutrition, personalized over time.**

    • 11. Social advertising

    Best visual narrative:

    **Photo → Understand → Track → Plan**

    Lead with the meal or product experience, not with a slow logo intro.

    12. Accessibility

    Maintain sufficient text/background contrast. Never rely on color alone to communicate nutrition state, warnings or achievement status.
