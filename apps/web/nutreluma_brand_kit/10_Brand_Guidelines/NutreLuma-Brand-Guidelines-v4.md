# NutreLuma Brand Guidelines v4 — Liquid Glass

## Brand direction
Premium, intelligent nutrition software with a luminous Liquid Glass visual system. Calm, precise, futuristic and human.

## Primary product icon
Use the selected Liquid Glass icon as the master. Keep the orbital ring, four-petal intelligence mark and central orb intact. Do not stretch, redraw or add extra effects.

## Tagline
**Nourish a brighter you.**

## Core palette
- Midnight Navy `#081020`
- Electric Blue `#2E63FF`
- Ice Cyan `#8EDCFF`
- Aurora Violet `#8B56FF`
- Solar Gold `#F2C14E`
- Frost White `#F5F8FF`

## Typography
Apple: SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text. Cross-platform fallback: Inter.

## Material rules
Deep navy foundations, translucent glass, restrained edge glow, blue/violet energy and gold only as a premium accent. Nutritional data must remain highly legible and accessible.

## App delivery
`04_App_Icons/iOS/AppIcon.appiconset` is ready for Xcode. Android includes density folders, Play Store 512 and adaptive-icon starter assets. Web/PWA includes favicon, Apple touch icon and manifest icons.
